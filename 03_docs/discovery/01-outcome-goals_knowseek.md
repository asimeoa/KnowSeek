# 🎯 01 – Outcome Goals: KnowSeek.ai

## 1️⃣ Primary Business Outcome (The "North Star")
**Goal:** Reduce technical uncertainty and search-waste.
> In 3 weeks, engineers will be able to verify technical specs across fragmented formats (PDF/Excel/Email) in under 30 seconds, resulting in zero usage of outdated "Lastenhefte".

## 2️⃣ Product Outcome (The "What")
**Goal:** Build a functional, trustworthy prototype.
* **Goal A (Search):** Achieving an 80% success rate in retrieving specific fastener data from an Excel/PDF hybrid source.
* **Goal B (Comparison):** Enabling a side-by-side "Conflict Resolution" UI where a user can settle a requirement dispute in 3 clicks.

## 3️⃣ Learning Outcome (The "How")
**Goal:** Personal growth in AI-Project Management.
* **Week 1:** Master local Data Ingestion (How to get PDF/Mail/Excel into a Vector DB).
* **Week 2:** Implement the "Human-in-the-Loop" logic in Lovable (User-Decision-Flow).
* **Week 3:** Refine the RAG-Output to ensure 100% citation accuracy (Source-Linking).

- [ ] UI shows all 4 modules (Part, Doc, Norm, Cost).
- [ ] PartSeek can find a fastener based on an internal part number.
- [ ] DocSeek can compare two versions of a "Volvo-style" requirement.
